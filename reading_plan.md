# Reading Plan for Financial Crisis Role-Play Simulation

Based on `lecture_summary/` analysis of all 18 files in `lecture_markdown/`.
Read in tier order; stop wherever your remaining time runs out.

## Tier 1 — Read first (operational for any crisis scenario)

| # | Lecture | Summary file | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lec-13 Crisis Communication | `lecture_summary/Lec-13-Crisis-Communication.md` | The actual script for the press-conference format: one voice, plain bad news, never a problem without a solution, don't over-promise. Relevant to every role, not just Press. |
| 2 | Lec-05 IMF & World Bank | `lecture_summary/Lec-05-IMF-World-Bank.md` | IMF lending-facility taxonomy (SBA/EFF/RFI/FCL etc.) and conditionality logic — directly answers "instrument used." |
| 3 | Lec-17 Early Warning Indicators | `lecture_summary/Lec-17-Early-Warning-Indicators.md` | Checklist (reserves, credit growth, credit-to-GDP gap, money-market stress) RBI/IMF roles cite as evidence. |
| 4 | Lec-03 Aggregates & Indicators | `lecture_summary/Lec-03-Aggregates-Indicators.md` | BoP, FX reserves, external debt, fiscal deficit vocabulary; India's own 1966 & 1991 BoP crises as precedent. |
| 5 | Lec-02 Monetary Policy | `lecture_summary/Lec-02-Monetary-Policy.md` | The actual toolkit (repo rate, CRR/SLR, forward guidance, UMPTs) RBI roles would announce as their response. |
