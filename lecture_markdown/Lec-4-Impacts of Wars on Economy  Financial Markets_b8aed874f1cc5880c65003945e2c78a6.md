1914: The Road to Destruction - World War-I
2
▪ The World has witnessed two World Wars so far: WW-I (28-Jul-1914 to 11-Nov-1918) &
WW-II (1-Sep-1939 to 2-Sep-1945)
▪ WW-I, also known as the Great War, was a global conflict that involved the Central Powers
(primarily Germany, Austria-Hungary, and the Ottoman Empire) against the Allied Powers
(primarily France, Great Britain, Russia, Italy, Japan, and later the U.S.), also first known as
Triple Entente the informal understanding between the Russian empire, the French Third
Republic, U.K. and Ireland
▪ Origin of the WW-I lay in shifting balance of power with the rise of Germany with decline of
Ottoman Empire, industrial competition, and imperialism. Strife in Balkan with
assassination of Gavrilo Princip on 28th June 1914, caused Austria & Hungary to blame
Serbia and declared a war against it.
▪ Main areas of conflict included Europe and the Middle East, parts of Africa and the Asia-
Pacific.
▪ In Europe, the trench warfare produced no winners and this prompted technological
innovations and a race for new arms –including machine guns, artillery, tanks, aircrafts and
even chemical weapon
▪ The 100-day offensive starting Aug. 1918 caused a collapse of the German front line.
Following the Vardar offensive, Bulgaria signed an armistice in late Sept. By early Nov., the
Ottoman Empire and Austria-Hungary had each signed armistices with the Allies, leaving
Germany isolated. Facing rebellion at home. Kaiser Wilhelm II abdicated on 9th Nov and
the war ended with the Armistice of 11th Nov 1918.
